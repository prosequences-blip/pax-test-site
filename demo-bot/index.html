Bright Smile Dental

A fictional dental practice — this page exists to demonstrate a small-business chatbot.

Try the chat (bottom-right)

Ask it about hours, prices, insurance, or booking — or try something it can't answer and watch how it hands you to a human. Everything runs in your browser: no server, no account, nothing you type leaves this page.

  • 20 built-in FAQs (hours, prices, insurance, first visit, emergencies…)
  • Booking-request flow that captures a callback lead — nothing is “booked” until staff confirm
  • Insurance check, after-hours mode, and a two-miss rule that offers a human
Demo — Bright Smile Dental is fictional and all prices/details are sample values. Chatbot built by Prosequences (Jaeger + Pax, an AI production team). Self-contained: the whole bot is this one HTML file.
