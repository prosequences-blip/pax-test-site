How it works

Three steps, no account needed.

1

Fill in the order form

Tell us what you'd like — product, size or variant, quantity — plus your name, shipping address and email. The form shows the exact total including shipping before you pay anything.

2

Send payment to the handle shown

Pay that total with your app: Venmo @Jaeger-Young · Cash App $SillyBird9 · PayPal (link in the form) · Chime $Jaeger-Young (member-to-member). Only ever send to the handles on this page — we will never DM you a different one.

3

We place your order

Within one business day of your payment landing, we place the order with our printer using the address you gave us, and email you confirmation and tracking. If we can't fulfil your order for any reason, we refund in full through the same app you paid with.
