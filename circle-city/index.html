Circle City braids three threads of the Prosequences vision into one shape. The circular city lays community out in rings instead of sprawl. The Wellness Wheel gives it eight spokes — the eight dimensions of a balanced life, one homestead for each. And the Ringing Cedars kin's-domain idea fills every spoke: a family's own plot of land, a living hedge for a fence, gardens laid out by permaculture rings, a pond, and cedar trees — land that feeds the people who tend it. Interactive districts are coming soon; for now, walk around it with your fingers.
